The situation

SnowShop is a small online store. The CEO, Priya, hired you — Maya, the Snowflake Architect — to build a safe, cheap, understandable data platform.

Your coworkers never change:

  • Alex — Analyst (charts, no secrets)
  • Jordan — Data Engineer (pipes, dbt, messy dock data)
  • Sam — Compliance (allowed to see sensitive fields for audits)

Later: Nora (partner store) and Dev (QA twin). Every lab is the next day of this same week. Read the story once before Lab 00.

Why roles are named FR_ and AR_ (not random)
PrefixStands forIn the story
FR_ Functional Role Job badge — who you are (Analyst, Engineer, Compliance)
AR_ Access Role Key-ring — which doors that job may open

Example: Alex wears FR_ANALYST, which includes the key-ring AR_CURATED_READ. Humans wear FR_. Permissions hang on AR_. That naming is intentional so you can read a role list and know the purpose.

How you learn each day Read that chapter in the story → open the lab → read orange story boxes → blue “Understand this” → Copy SQL → run in Snowsight → say the soundbite as Maya.
