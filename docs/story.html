SnowShop: the story of your course

Read this when labs feel abstract. You are the architect for a tiny shop called SnowShop. Each lab is the next chapter. SQL lives in the lab pages — this page is only the story.

Chapter 00 — Build the workshop

You open a private workshop building (ARCH_INTERVIEW), buy a small generator (WH_ARCH_XS, and become workshop manager ROLE_ARCH_LAB_ADMIN. Rooms: RAW (loading dock), CURATED (clean shelves), SECURE (policy office), MART (report wall), etc.

Chapter 01 — Keys for three jobs

Five customers sit in the vault with emails and SSN. You invent job badges (Analyst, Engineer, Compliance) and key-rings (safe window / vault / PII). Analysts look through a glass window — vault stays locked. Red “Insufficient privileges” = happy ending.

Chapter 02 — The night clerk who only files new papers

Orders arrive every day. Instead of rewriting the whole order book each night, the clerk (dbt incremental merge) only adds new orders and updates changed ones. Day 1 five orders. Day 2 two new + one status change → seven clean rows.

Chapter 03 — Magic ink and velvet ropes

Even at the safe table, email is written in magic ink: Analysts see ***@…, Compliance sees the real address. Velvet ropes (row access) hide non-US rows from Analysts. Same SELECT, different costume → different reality.

Chapter 04 — Dock → kitchen → dining room

Bronze = messy dock (RAW). Silver = cleaned kitchen (CURATED). Gold = plated dishes for guests (MART). Data Vault is another kitchen layout for huge history — you learn when to choose it, not build a cathedral.

Chapter 05 — The conveyor belt vs the sous-chef vs the recipe book

Stream + task = conveyor belt that moves new dock boxes into the kitchen on a schedule. Dynamic table = sous-chef who keeps a dish fresh with a promised lag. dbt = recipe book the kitchen team versions in Git. You pick the tool for the job.

Chapter 06 — Trucks unload at the dock

Files land on a stage (truck bay). COPY INTO pours them into a landing table. Snowpipe is the same pour — automated when the truck beeps at the cloud door.

Chapter 07 — X-ray the slow order

Query Profile is the X-ray: where did time go? Spilling to disk? Waiting for a bigger generator? Scale up = bigger engine. Scale out = more engines in parallel for many users.

Chapter 08 — Sort the warehouse aisles

Clustering is labeling aisles by date so you don’t walk every shelf for “last 7 days.” Search optimization is a special index for “find this one needle” lookups — costs money, use on purpose.

Chapter 09 — Undo and photocopy without photocopy paper

Time Travel / UNDROP = rewind a mistake. Zero-copy clone = instant practice copy of the shop for QA — no full data copy bill for the first second of existence.
